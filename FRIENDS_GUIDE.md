# Athena (Trackboard) — Friend's Guide & Onboarding

Welcome to **Athena** (Trackboard)! This platform is your personal, private career departure board and intelligent opportunity tracker. It replaces scattered job spreadsheets, unorganized LeetCode grinding, and generic resume tailoring with an integrated, automated workflow.

**Live Application URL**: [https://athena-phi-one.vercel.app](https://athena-phi-one.vercel.app)

---

## 1. Access & Logging In

1. Open **[https://athena-phi-one.vercel.app](https://athena-phi-one.vercel.app)**.
2. If you are not signed in, go to `/login` (or click your profile icon in the top right corner).
3. **Enter your email address** (e.g. `manshi@gmail.com` or `prerna@gmail.com`):
   - Your session is saved securely in a 30-day browser cookie.
   - All your applications, resume tailoring, target keywords, and match queues are strictly isolated to your own user account.

---

## 2. Setting Up Your Profile (Web UI — No Code Required)

Manage your entire career profile directly from the browser at **`/profile`**:

1. **Go to `/profile`** (click **Profile** in the top navigation bar).
2. **Select Your Career Track**:
   - 💻 **Engineering, Tech & AI**: Designed for Software Development Engineers (SDEs), Backend Developers, Full Stack Developers, and AI/ML Engineers. Includes interactive DSA Pattern Workbenches, LeetCode drill engines, and technical recruiter audits.
   - 📊 **Business, Operations & Analytics**: Designed for Business Analysts, Operations Managers, Product Analysts, and Corporate Strategy roles. Automatically customizes the UI to focus 100% on job matching, pipeline tracking, and recruiter tailoring without showing coding theory.
3. **Set Your Target Roles & Keywords**:
   - **Target Titles**: 
     - *Tech Track*: `SDE, Backend Engineer, Full Stack Developer, AI Engineer`
     - *Business Track*: `Business Analyst, Operations Manager, Product Analyst, Operations Specialist`
   - **Target Skills & Keywords**:
     - *Tech Track*: `Python, FastAPI, PyTorch, Docker, PostgreSQL, LangChain, React, LLM, Redis`
     - *Business Track*: `SQL, Excel, Operations, Tableau, Agile, Jira, Stakeholder Management, Process Optimization`
   - **Preferred Locations & CTC**: e.g., `Bengaluru, Mumbai, Remote, India` / `18 LPA` / `2 years experience`.
4. **Upload Your Master Resume**:
   - Upload your PDF resume or paste your experience text. Athena extracts your bullets and skills directly into your private profile.
5. **Click `⚡ Match Jobs for My Profile`**:
   - Athena scans the live job catalog against your target keywords, generates personalized match scores (0-100%), and populates your private **`/jobs`** queue!

---

## 3. Reviewing Your Matched Roles & Resume Tailoring

1. Go to **`/jobs`** to view jobs scored specifically for your profile with BM25 keyword matching and recruiter fit evaluations.
2. Click **Tailor** on any role (`/jobs/{id}/tailor`) to launch the **5-Pillar Recruiter Audit**:
   - **10-Second Attention Test**: Identifies your standout proof points and flags forgettable filler.
   - **Recruiter Mindset Breakdown**: Reveals how a recruiter at that specific company evaluates candidates.
   - **ATS Visibility Engine**: Recommends key verbs and natural keyword placements to maximize ATS pass rates.
   - **Impact Statement Rebuilder**: Rewrites passive bullets into high-impact `[Action Verb] + [Context] + [Measurable Metric]` statements.
   - **Market Positioning Rewrite**: Customizes your professional summary and headline for the company's culture.
3. Generate and download a tailored, single-page PDF formatted for ATS scanners.

## 4. Pipeline Tracking (`/pipeline`)

Track every job application across standard Kanban stages:
`Prepared` ➔ `Submitted` ➔ `Acknowledged` ➔ `Screening` ➔ `Assessment` ➔ `Interview` ➔ `Offer`.

Update statuses manually or let automated inbox monitoring track confirmation emails.
