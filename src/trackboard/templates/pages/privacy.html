Privacy

Last updated 26 September 2026.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and display name from Google sign-in.
  • Profile: the résumé text you provide, your target roles, keywords, locations, and optional contact fields you choose to add (phone, LinkedIn, GitHub) for copy-ready application chips.
  • Activity: jobs you mark applied or dismissed, application stages you record, practice attempts and reviews.
  • Technical: standard server logs, and per-IP counters used for rate limiting that expire within an hour.

Where it lives

In a single SQLite database on the hosting provider (Vercel). There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel, and no third-party tracker on any page. YouTube videos on pattern pages load only after you press play, through YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain.

AI processing

When you run a match, a tailoring interview or a LinkedIn audit, the relevant text is sent to a language-model provider (Google Gemini, with fallbacks via OpenRouter). Names, emails and phone numbers are redacted before sending when LLM_REDACT_PII is on, which is the default. Job descriptions are wrapped as untrusted data so they can't act as instructions.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. Sandbox accounts are deleted at sign-out or within two days. You can delete your account and every row tied to it at any time from your profile; deletion is immediate and not reversible.

Sharing

We don't sell or share personal data. The only third parties that see any of it are Google (sign-in) and the model providers above, for the single request you trigger.
