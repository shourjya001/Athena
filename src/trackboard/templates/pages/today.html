How it works

Three agents, one SQLite file, nothing hidden.

See live status →
Ingest

A scout agent polls public ATS APIs nightly, de-duplicates by company + title + location, and closes a role after it vanishes from three consecutive runs.

Score

A BM25 shortlist, then a recruiter-style pass across four pillars: direct skills, transferable depth, adjacent tooling, and quantified impact. If the model is offline you see "Unscored", not a made-up number.

Prepare

Tailored résumés are selected from your own bullet bank and rejected if a contact field goes missing. Practice is a spaced-repetition queue across 26 patterns.

Questions people ask

Straight answers, including the ones that aren't flattering.

Where do the jobs come from?

From the public JSON APIs that companies expose through their applicant-tracking systems — Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and Oracle — plus a small set of hand-checked careers pages for companies that don't use one. Nothing is scraped from LinkedIn, Naukri or Instahyre.

Are the links real?

Every "Open on company site" link goes to the employer's own page. Before redirecting, Athena checks known ATS hosts for a closed posting and removes it if so. A role is also closed automatically after it disappears from three consecutive nightly runs.

How is the score calculated?

A BM25 shortlist against your résumé text first, then a language model rates the top candidates on direct skills (40%), transferable depth (30%), adjacent tooling (20%) and quantified impact (10%). You see the reasoning, the strengths and the gaps. If the model is unavailable you see "Unscored", never a placeholder.

Will it write things on my résumé that I didn't do?

No. Tailoring selects and reorders bullets from your own bank. When a job asks for something you lack, a short interview asks how you've actually touched it, and you edit the result before it's saved.

Does it auto-apply for me?

No, on purpose. Auto-apply bots violate most job boards' terms and get accounts banned. Athena gives you copy-ready fields and a tailored PDF; you press submit.

What does it cost, and what happens to my data?

It's free while it's in early access. Your data is your email, your résumé text and your activity, stored in one database with no trackers. You can delete everything from your profile in one click. Full details on the privacy page.

About the build

Athena is a Python, FastAPI, Jinja and HTMX app on a single SQLite file, with no client-side framework and no ORM. Eight small agents read and write the same tables instead of calling each other, which is why it runs on a free serverless plan. The status page shows every agent's last run. Built by Shourjya Hazra.
